# Source/provenance

CC0 models and image derivatives from the [BFjord public asset catalog](https://github.com/brianwang9100/bfjord-tools/tree/main/assets/BFjordTools/asset-catalog/Assets/Bwork/ThirdParty/PolyHaven). Supplier: [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) (CC0 1.0); original supplier pages: [fern_02](https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02), [shrub_03](https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_03), [pine_sapling_small](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_sapling_small), [rock_moss_set_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_moss_set_01), [forest_floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor). BFjord's [asset license](https://github.com/brianwang9100/bfjord-tools/blob/main/docs/ASSET_LICENSE.md) explicitly covers its prepared Poly Haven derivatives under CC0 and identifies Forest Floor as eye-candy.xyz / Poly Haven. Models imported as FBX; texture images connected to principled shaders preserving their original albedo, normal and cutout alpha. FBX importer placeholders are not used as scene materials.
